# Roaming Agreements Summary

## 1. Partners and zones

Altair holds bilateral roaming agreements with 112 partner networks across three zones. Zone 1 (North America)
is included in Unlimited Max; Zones 2 (Europe) and 3 (rest of world) are billed per day pass.

## 2. What counts as roaming revenue

Roaming revenue reported to finance is **retail outbound roaming billed to Altair subscribers** travelling
abroad. Wholesale inbound roaming — partner subscribers using Altair's network, settled with partners through
the clearing house — is **excluded from roaming revenue unless a report explicitly asks to include it**.
Including wholesale roughly doubles the figure and mixes retail and wholesale margins.

## 3. Seasonality

Roaming usage peaks in July and August; day-pass revenue is recognised in the month the pass is used.

## 4. Settlement

Wholesale inbound records arrive in TAP files up to seven days late and are reconciled monthly with each
partner.
