# Credit and Collections Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Altair collects billed charges, when overdue balances are written off and how
autopay is promoted. Collections are fair, explainable and decided by people.

## 2. Payment methods

Subscribers pay by autopay, card, cash at a store or bank transfer. Autopay invoices settle on the due date;
other methods take longer on average. Autopay rate is the share of active subscribers with a recurring
automatic payment on file.

## 3. Overdue balances

An invoice unpaid 30 days after its due date enters collections. After 90 days and two contact attempts the
line is suspended; after 120 days the balance is written off as bad debt and the line is disconnected
involuntarily.

## 4. Bad debt

Bad debt ratio is billed charges (including device installments) written off, divided by total billed. Cash
payers and handset financing cohorts carry most of the write-offs.

## 5. Customer contact data

Collectors contact subscribers using details in the subscriber master. These details are personal information
and are masked in analytical data products for anyone without PII clearance.

## 6. Reporting

Collections performance is reported monthly by payment method, segment and region in aggregate.
