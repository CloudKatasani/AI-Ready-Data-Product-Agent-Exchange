# Patient Financial Services and Collections Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how Crestview bills patients for their share of the cost of care, offers payment plans and
financial assistance, and pursues unpaid self-pay balances fairly and lawfully.

## 2. Statements and payment plans

Patients receive a statement after the payer has adjudicated the claim. Balances can be paid in full or through an
interest-free payment plan of up to 24 months. Patient financial services staff may set up a plan on request;
plans above 24 months need supervisor approval.

## 3. Financial assistance

Uninsured and underinsured patients with household income below 400% of the federal poverty guideline are eligible
for financial assistance. Applications are accepted at any point in the billing cycle, including after a balance is
placed with a collection agency. Eligibility decisions are made by a named counsellor, never by an automated score.

## 4. Collection actions

No extraordinary collection action is taken within 120 days of the first statement or while a financial assistance
application is pending. Collection actions, payment plans and financial assistance determinations are made by a
named human in patient financial services; agents never recommend collection action against an individual patient
(control CTL-HC-012).

## 5. Reporting

Self-pay balances, payment plan adherence and financial assistance volumes are reported in aggregate by market and
financial class. Individual patient balances are visible only in the patient accounting system to staff working an
assigned account.
