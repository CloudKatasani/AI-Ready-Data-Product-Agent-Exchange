# Nurse Staffing Plan and Labour Standards

## 1. Purpose

The nurse staffing committee approves a staffing plan for every inpatient care unit each year. This document sets the
measures used to monitor the plan and the thresholds that trigger action. It underpins the Nurse Staffing & Capacity
data product, which is in development.

## 2. Census and occupancy

The **midnight census** is the number of patients occupying a bed on a care unit at midnight. **Bed occupancy** is
census divided by staffed (not licensed) beds. Units above 90% occupancy for a week are reviewed for surge staffing
(BR-HC-009).

## 3. Nursing hours per patient day

**Nursing hours per patient day (HPPD)** are productive nursing hours worked divided by patient days. Each unit type has
a plan HPPD: intensive care 16, step-down 11, maternity and pediatrics 10, medical-surgical, telemetry and behavioral
health 8. Hours versus plan compares hours worked with plan hours for the actual census.

## 4. Overtime and contract labour

Overtime above 8% of hours worked on a unit for a month is reviewed by the unit manager. Contract labour — agency and
travel nurses — above 8% of nursing hours on a unit for a quarter requires a recruitment plan approved by the chief
nursing officer (BR-HC-010). Riverside Medical Center's intensive care unit exceeded this threshold in the third quarter
of 2026.

## 5. Data use

Staffing measures are reported by facility, market, unit type and care unit. Individual staff schedules and timecards
remain in the workforce management system.
