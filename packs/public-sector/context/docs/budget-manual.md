# Budget, Fiscal Year and Grants Administration Manual

## 1. Fiscal year

Westland County's **fiscal year** runs from 1 July to 30 June and is named for the year in which it ends: FY2027 runs
from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027. Budget figures are reported for the **current fiscal year to date** unless a report
names another fiscal year (for example FY2026). Calendar-year figures are never mixed with fiscal-year budgets.

## 2. Adopted budget and actuals

The Board of Supervisors adopts the budget by department; the budget office spreads it by month. Actual expenditure
is posted from the general ledger when each month closes.

- **Budget variance** is actual expenditure minus adopted budget, as a share of adopted budget. A positive variance
  is an overspend.
- **Budget execution** is actual expenditure as a share of adopted budget.

A department more than 5% over budget for the fiscal year to date reports to the Board with a corrective plan. In
the first quarter of FY2027 Public Works was well over budget after contractor pothole patching carried over from
spring, while Community Development underspent with plan reviewer posts vacant.

## 3. Positions and vacancy

Authorised positions are set in the adopted budget. The **vacancy rate** is authorised positions minus filled
positions, divided by authorised positions, averaged over the months in scope. Eligibility Services vacancies rose
through 2026 and are the main driver of slower benefit processing in Riverside.

## 4. Grants

The county receives federal, state and foundation grants for a fixed award period. Funds are drawn down as eligible
expenditure is incurred.

- The **grant drawdown rate** is dollars drawn down divided by dollars awarded. It covers **active grants** (award
  period not yet ended) unless a report asks to include closed grants.
- A grant is **at risk** when its drawdown trails the elapsed share of its award period by more than 20 percentage
  points. At-risk grants are reviewed monthly so that funds are not returned unspent.

Transportation grants are drawing down slowly while projects wait on right-of-way acquisition.

## 5. Public records

Budget figures given to the Board must be reproducible from a certified product version, metric definition and query
in response to a public records request.
