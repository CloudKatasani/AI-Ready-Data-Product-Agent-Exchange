# 311 Service Level Standard

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines how Westland County measures and publishes the service residents receive through 311 —
by phone, the 311 mobile app, the web portal or walk-in at a service center. It applies to every department that
receives 311 requests and to the Service Requests (311) data product.

## 2. What a request is

A **311 service request** is a non-emergency request or report from a resident, such as a pothole, a missed trash
pickup or a streetlight out. When residents report the same issue more than once, later reports are **merged as
duplicates** into the first (parent) request and closed at the moment of merge.

Merged duplicates are **excluded** from 311 volume, resolution time, SLA attainment and satisfaction unless a report
explicitly asks to include them. Including them would understate resolution time, because a duplicate closes the
moment it is merged.

## 3. Service level agreements by request type

A request is **on time** when it is closed within the SLA for its request type:

| Request type | Department | SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Water leak | Water Utility | 1 day |
| Missed trash pickup | Solid Waste | 2 days |
| Benefits inquiry | Eligibility Services | 2 days |
| Noise complaint | Code Enforcement | 3 days |
| Pothole | Public Works | 5 days |
| Graffiti removal | Parks & Facilities | 5 days |
| Streetlight out | Public Works | 7 days |
| Tree or debris | Parks & Facilities | 7 days |
| Abandoned vehicle | Code Enforcement | 10 days |
| Housing inspection | Code Enforcement | 14 days |

**SLA attainment** is closed requests resolved within their SLA divided by closed requests. The county target is
at least 85% county-wide.

## 4. Resolution time

**311 resolution time** is measured in calendar days from creation to closure; open requests are excluded.
**Pothole repair time** is the resolution time of pothole requests and is the figure residents see on the 311 app.

## 5. Reopens and satisfaction

A request is **reopened** when the resident reports that the issue was not fixed. Every closed request receives a
five-point post-closure survey. **Citizen satisfaction** is responses scoring 4 or 5 divided by all responses;
requests without a response are excluded. The survey method and response weighting are under review in 2026.

## 6. Open data

311 volumes, resolution times and SLA attainment are published monthly at district level on the county open-data
portal. Caller names are never published.
