# Permit Review and Inspection Standards

## 1. Purpose

These standards set the review targets, inspection practice and fee basis for building, trade and right-of-way
permits issued by Westland County Community Development, and how permit turnaround is reported.

## 2. Review tracks

Every permit application is assigned a **review track** when the submittal is complete:

- **Over the counter** — simple trade and sign permits issued the same day at the permit counter or online.
- **Standard** — residential and minor commercial work reviewed by one plans examiner.
- **Major** — new commercial buildings and multi-discipline projects needing structural, fire and civil review.

## 3. Review targets and turnaround

| Track | Target from complete submittal |
|---|---|
| Over the counter | 1 day |
| Standard | 20 calendar days |
| Major | 45 calendar days |

**Permit turnaround** is calendar days from a complete submittal to the permit decision (issue or denial). Because
over-the-counter permits are issued the same day, they are **excluded** from turnaround and timeliness unless a report
explicitly asks to include them; including them would hide plan-review delays. Commercial building permits take
longest. In the third quarter of 2026, two plan reviewer vacancies slowed Central district reviews.

## 4. Inspections

Field inspections — footing, framing, electrical and plumbing rough-in, and final — are booked by the permit holder.
The **first inspection pass rate** is the share of inspected permits whose first inspection passed. Failed
inspections are re-booked after correction.

## 5. Fees

The permit fee is a base fee of 85 dollars plus 1.1% of declared construction valuation. Fee schedules are adopted
by the Board each fiscal year.

## 6. Records and open data

Permit records are retained permanently. Applicant names and emails are personal data and are masked in analytical
products; monthly permit counts and turnaround are published as open data.
