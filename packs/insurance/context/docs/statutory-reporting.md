# Statutory Reporting Application Guide — Loss, Expense and Combined Ratios

## 1. Purpose

This guide sets out how Sentinel Mutual applies statutory accounting definitions to the ratios it reports to the
board, the state supervisory council and every dashboard and agent answer. One set of definitions applies to the
annual statement, the quarterly underwriting result pack and the certified Underwriting Result product.

## 2. Loss ratio

The loss ratio is incurred losses divided by earned premium, never written premium. Incurred losses are paid losses
plus case reserves on claims with a loss date in the accident period. Loss adjustment expense is reported separately
as the LAE ratio.

Catastrophe losses are identified by the catastrophe code list: any claim carrying a code from the list is a
catastrophe claim. The reported loss ratio excludes catastrophe-coded losses and shows them separately as
catastrophe points, so that attritional performance can be compared across periods. The all-in loss ratio is the
ex-catastrophe loss ratio plus catastrophe points and is used only when explicitly requested.

## 3. Accident period

"This year" in a loss ratio means accident year to date: losses on claims with a loss date from 1 January, against
premium earned in the same months. Quarterly figures use the accident quarter.

## 4. Expense, LAE and combined ratios

The expense ratio is commission and other underwriting expense divided by earned premium. Contingent commission
accruals are booked in the quarter they are earned. The LAE ratio is loss adjustment expense divided by earned
premium.

The combined ratio adds the all-in loss ratio, the LAE ratio and the expense ratio. A combined ratio below 100%
indicates an underwriting profit.

## 5. Claim frequency

Claim frequency is the number of claims per 100 policy-years of earned exposure, annualised from the exposure months
in the period.

## 6. Catastrophe code list

Catastrophe risk opens a code when an event is designated and closes it 30 days after the event ends. Claims are
coded by footprint and loss date. The 2025–2026 list includes the February 2025 ice storm, the Gulf hurricane of
September 2025, the October 2025 wildfire complex, the January 2026 coastal storm, the May 2026 hail outbreak, the
Atlantic hurricane of August 2026 and the September 2026 wildfire.

## 7. Lineage and certification

Every reported ratio must trace to a certified product version, metric definition and the governed query that
produced it. Annual statement lines group Sentinel products for statutory schedules; incurred losses by accident year
support the loss development triangles filed each year.
