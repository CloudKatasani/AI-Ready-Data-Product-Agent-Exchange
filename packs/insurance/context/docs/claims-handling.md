# Claims Handling Standard

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines how Sentinel Mutual handles claims from first notice of loss to closure and how claims
performance is measured. It applies to every adjuster, handling team and vendor.

## 2. First notice of loss and assignment

A claim is created at first notice of loss (FNOL) through the agency, the mobile app, the contact centre or, during a
declared catastrophe, the catastrophe response team. Claims are assigned to a handling team: fast track, auto physical
damage, property field, casualty, complex and large loss, or catastrophe response.

## 3. Open claims and cycle time

A claim is open while its status is Open or Reopened and a case reserve remains. Claims fully paid and awaiting only a
subrogation recovery are not open. Cycle time is measured from first notice of loss to closure, on closed claims only.

## 4. Severity

Claim severity is the average incurred loss per claim with a loss date in the period. Loss adjustment expense is
excluded. Catastrophe claims are excluded from severity unless explicitly requested, because a single event would
otherwise distort attritional trends.

## 5. Large-loss review

Claims with incurred losses of $50,000 or more are large losses. Open injury large losses are reviewed quarterly by a
claims manager and escalated to the reserving committee when reserves change by more than 25%.

## 6. Subrogation

Subrogation is pursued on every claim where a third party is liable. Recoveries are credited when received and
reported against paid losses on eligible claims that have closed.

## 7. Special investigations

Claims that match fraud indicators — staged accident patterns, inflated estimates, provider billing anomalies — are
referred to the special investigations unit (SIU). A referral is not a finding of fraud. Referral rates are reported
in aggregate only; whether a named claimant has been referred is never disclosed.

## 8. Authority

Coverage, liability and settlement decisions are made and documented by a named, licensed adjuster with authority for
the amount. Analytical products and agents explain claim experience but never decide coverage or settle a claim.
