# Loss Reserving Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how Sentinel Mutual sets, reviews and reports loss reserves. It applies to case reserves set by
adjusters, IBNR set by the reserving committee and the actuarial indication used to test adequacy.

## 2. Case reserves

Case reserves are set by the adjuster at first notice of loss and reviewed at least every 30 days. Reserves are
reported at each month-end valuation; closed claims carry no case reserve. Balances drawn from month-end valuations
are averaged across the valuations in a period, never summed.

## 3. IBNR

IBNR is set quarterly by the reserving committee as a factor by line of business and allocated to open claims in
proportion to their case reserve. Long-tail lines (general liability and workers compensation) carry the highest
factors.

## 4. Adequacy

Carried reserves (case plus IBNR) are compared with the actuarial central indication each month. Reserve adequacy
below 95% for any line is escalated to the reserving committee with a recommended strengthening.

## 5. Personal data on the reserving extract

The reserving extract carries the claimant's name and phone number so actuaries can reconcile large losses with the
claims file. Both are personal data: they must be masked for every role except the data steward before the Loss
Reserves product is certified.

## 6. Decisions

Reserve changes are decided by adjusters within authority and by the reserving committee above it. Agents may report
reserve balances and adequacy but never set or change a reserve.
