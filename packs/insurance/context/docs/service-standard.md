# Policyholder Service Standard

## 1. Purpose

This standard sets how Sentinel Mutual serves policyholders across the phone, chat, the mobile app, email and agency
offices, and how service is measured.

## 2. Measures

Net promoter score is the percentage of promoters (scores 9–10) minus the percentage of detractors (0–6) among
policyholders surveyed after a contact. First-contact resolution is the share of contacts resolved without the
policyholder contacting us again on the same matter. A complaint is a contact in which the policyholder expresses
dissatisfaction that requires a response.

## 3. Complaints

Complaints are acknowledged within two business days and resolved within 30 days. Complaint registers are available
to the state supervisory council on request.

## 4. Catastrophe surge

During a declared catastrophe the service centre extends hours, routes claim-status calls to the catastrophe response
team and suspends outbound sales calls in the footprint.
