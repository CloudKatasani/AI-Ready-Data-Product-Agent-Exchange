# Underwriting Guidelines 2026

## 1. Eligibility and binding authority

Personal lines risks are eligible when the applicant has no more than two at-fault losses in three years. Commercial
risks above $5M insured value require referral to a senior underwriter. Binding authority sits with licensed
underwriters and appointed agencies within their limits; no analytical product or agent may bind, price or decline a
risk.

## 2. Catastrophe accumulation

Property insured value in hurricane and wildfire zones must not exceed 40% of total in-force property insured value.
New property business in those zones is suspended when the share passes 38%.

## 3. Renewals and retention

Renewal underwriting starts 60 days before expiry. Retention is measured on policies reaching their renewal date:
policies renewed divided by policies due. Mid-term cancellations are tracked separately and excluded from the
denominator. A policy in force has started its current term and has not lapsed or been cancelled.

## 4. Submissions and quote-to-bind

Quote-to-bind is bound submissions divided by quoted submissions, by received date. Submissions declined before a
quote are excluded. New business premium excludes rewrites of existing business and reinstatements.

## 5. Agency service standard

A complete submission is quoted within 3 business days. Agencies averaging longer are reviewed quarterly with the
distribution team. The direct digital channel quotes personal lines in real time.

## 6. Rate changes

Rate changes are filed by product and state. The 2026 personal auto filings average +6% and commercial lines are
holding rate in a hardening market.
