# Harbor Rewards Programme Rules

## 1. Membership

Any adult may join Harbor Rewards in store, online or in the app. Members earn one point per dollar of net
sales and receive tier benefits based on annual spend: Bronze, Silver (over $750) and Gold (over $2,000).

## 2. Active members

A member is **active** when they have made a purchase or redeemed points in the last 12 months. Members who
have not are **lapsed**; their points expire 24 months after their last activity. App adoption, churn risk and
points liability are measured over active members only.

## 3. Points

One point is worth one cent when redeemed. **Points liability** is the value of unredeemed points held by active
members and is accrued monthly by Finance.

## 4. App adoption

**App adoption** is the share of active members enrolled in the mobile app. App members receive personalised
offers and digital receipts; adoption is a priority in every region and is highest in Pacific.

## 5. Churn risk

The churn risk model scores each active member 0–100 for the likelihood they lapse in the next 12 months.
Scores of 60 or more are High risk. The model card is in review; scores must not be used to deny benefits or
service to any individual member.

## 6. Member data

Member names, emails, phone numbers and addresses are personal information. Analytical products and agents
report members only in aggregate — by region, tier or status — and never produce contact lists.
