# Sales Reporting Standard

## 1. Purpose and scope

This standard defines how Harbor & Pine reports sales, margin and transactions for stores and the web store. It
applies to every management report, investor figure and AI agent answer that quotes net sales, gross margin,
units, transactions or basket value. Figures are produced from the certified Store & Digital Sales data product.

## 2. Net sales

**Net sales** are sales after line discounts and markdowns, excluding sales tax, gift card sales and services
such as delivery fees. Returns are reported separately in the returns product and are not netted from net sales
in management reporting. Sales lines dated before a store's opening date are pre-opening test transactions and
are always excluded.

## 3. Comparable stores

A **comparable store** has been open for at least thirteen full months at the reporting date and is not closed
or trading from a reduced footprint for remodel. Sales KPIs — net sales, gross margin, units, transactions,
basket value and the channel and promotional shares — are reported on comparable stores so that growth is not
flattered by new openings. A report or answer may include all stores when the reader explicitly asks for it,
and must then say so. Eight Southwest stores opened on 15 September 2025 and become comparable in October 2026.

## 4. Gross margin

**Gross margin** is net sales less cost of goods sold at weighted average cost. The gross margin rate divides
gross margin by net sales and is always computed from the sums; it is never an average of store or category
rates.

## 5. Transactions and basket value

A **transaction** is one till or web-store checkout. **Average basket value** is net sales divided by the number
of distinct transactions. Units per transaction is reported alongside it in weekly trading packs.

## 6. Channels

Every sale carries a channel: In-store, Online (delivered) or Click & collect. Online and click & collect sales
are attributed to the fulfilling store, or for ship-to-home orders the member's nearest store, so that channel
sales roll up to store regions. **Digital sales share** is online plus click & collect net sales divided by net
sales.

## 7. Loyalty identification

A sale is **loyalty-identified** when a member scans a card or the app at checkout. The identified share of net
sales measures how much trading the loyalty programme can see and is a lead indicator for personalisation.

## 8. Reporting cadence

Trading is reported daily by store and region, weekly by department and category, and quarterly to the board.
Quarterly figures use complete fiscal quarters; the fiscal year is the calendar year.
