# Store Inventory and Shrink Standard

## 1. Purpose and scope

This standard defines how store inventory, availability and shrink are measured. It covers the perpetual
inventory record, in-stock and days-of-supply measures, cycle counts and the shrink reserve.

## 2. In-stock

An item is **in stock** in a store on a day when its on-hand units in the perpetual inventory record are greater
than zero at the end of the day. The **in-stock rate** is in-stock store-item-days divided by store-item-days.
The target is 95% or better; below 92% for a week triggers a replenishment review with the inventory planning
team.

## 3. Days of supply

**Days of supply** is on-hand units divided by the trailing average daily rate of sale, measured only on
in-stock positions. Healthy cover for everyday items is 10 to 20 days; seasonal items are planned separately.

## 4. Replenishment incidents

On 20 August 2026 a release of the replenishment system suppressed automatic store orders for a subset of items
until 27 August. In-stock rates fell during that week and recovered once orders were re-released. Analysts
comparing August with other months should call this out.

## 5. Cycle counts

Each store category is fully counted on a **6-, 12- or 18-month cycle** set by its shrink risk. A category is
overdue when its last full count plus its cycle is before the reporting date. Counts scoring 70 or more on the
loss-prevention risk score are recounted within 30 days.

## 6. Shrink

**Shrink** is the gap between system and counted inventory value caused by theft, damage, administrative error or
vendor fraud. The **shrink rate** is shrink value divided by system value counted. A count is within tolerance
when shrink is 2% of system value or less. The shrink reserve is trued up quarterly from counts.

## 7. Hot spots

Stores with a shrink rate more than twice the company rate are hot spots and receive a loss-prevention visit
and a full recount of high-risk categories within 30 days.
