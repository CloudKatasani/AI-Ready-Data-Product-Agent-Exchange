# Supplier Quality Manual (SQM-3)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This manual sets the quality and delivery expectations for suppliers of materials and components to Forgepoint
plants, and how supplier performance is measured on the supplier scorecard.

## 2. On time in full

**On time in full (OTIF)** is measured per goods receipt against the promise date on the purchase order and the
full ordered quantity. A supplier below **90% OTIF** for a quarter receives a **supplier corrective action
request (SCAR)**.

When a buyer pulls in a promise date after the supplier confirmed the order, the receipt is flagged as
rescheduled. Rescheduled receipts are **excluded from supplier OTIF**, because the supplier is measured against a
date it never accepted. They are reported separately to the buying team.

## 3. Incoming inspection and PPM

**Supplier PPM** is the number of parts rejected at incoming inspection per million parts received. Targets are
500 PPM for machined parts and 250 PPM for electronic components. Parts that fail later in production are charged
back separately and do not change PPM.

## 4. Lead time

Supplier lead time is the days from purchase order to goods receipt. Commodity managers review lead time trends
quarterly.

## 5. Confidentiality

Supplier prices, standard costs, tax identifiers and bank details are commercially confidential. Scorecards report
delivery and quality performance only.

## 6. Corrective actions

Approving a SCAR response, a deviation or a supplier change is a decision for the commodity manager and supplier
quality engineer. No automated score or agent approves corrective actions.
