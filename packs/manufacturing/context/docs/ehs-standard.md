# Environment, Health & Safety Reporting Standard

## 1. Purpose and scope

This standard defines how Forgepoint plants record safety incidents, measure safety performance and report energy
and emissions.

## 2. Incident severity

Incidents are recorded as **near miss**, **first aid**, **recordable** or **lost time**. Recordable incidents follow
OSHA-style definitions; lost-time incidents are also recordable. First-aid cases and near misses are not
recordable.

## 3. Safety rates

**TRIR** (total recordable incident rate) is recordable incidents per 200,000 hours worked — roughly 100
full-time employees for a year. **LTIR** is lost-time incidents per 200,000 hours. Hours worked come from the plant
headcount and the standard monthly hours, lower in the August and December shutdown months. Near-miss reporting is
a leading indicator: more reports are better. In 2026 the Oakhurst plant recorded a run of hand injuries at the
forging press.

## 4. Personal data

The name of an injured person is personal data. It is held in the incident record, masked for everyone without PII
clearance and never used in analytics.

## 5. Energy and emissions

Scope 2 emissions use **location-based** grid emission factors per plant, refreshed annually. Energy per unit
divides metered kWh by good units only.
