# Inventory and Materials Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets how Forgepoint plants hold and measure raw materials, purchased components, work in progress,
finished goods and MRO spares.

## 2. Valuation

Inventory is valued at the standard unit cost in the material master. Standard costs are trade secrets and are
never shown at material level outside finance and the data steward.

## 3. Days of supply and turns

**Days of supply** is on-hand value divided by daily usage value. **Inventory turns** is annualised usage value
divided by on-hand value (365 ÷ days of supply). Both are measured on **active materials only**. Obsolete stock
awaiting disposal has no usage and would make cover look far longer than it is; it is reported separately unless a
question asks to include it.

## 4. Stockouts and safety stock

A **stockout** is a material-plant-day with nothing on hand. Each material carries a safety stock expressed in days
of usage; a position below it is flagged for the planner. In September 2026 a carrier failure left the Dunmore
Flats plant short of purchased components.

## 5. Review thresholds

Active A-class materials above 45 days of supply are reviewed at the monthly inventory meeting. MRO spares are held
to criticality-based cover set with maintenance and are reviewed separately.

## 6. Boundaries

Placing purchase orders, moving stock and writing off obsolete material are planner and finance decisions made in
the ERP.
