# Control of Nonconforming Output and Field Quality (QP-8.7)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure implements ISO 9001 clause 8.7 for Forgepoint plants. It covers inspection lots, first-pass yield,
nonconformance reports (NCRs), the material review board and the cost of poor quality, and it sets how field
warranty claims are measured.

## 2. Inspection and first-pass yield

Every production lot is sampled and inspected before release. A lot **passes first time** only when the sample has
no defects. **First-pass yield** is the share of lots accepted at their first inspection. Lots released by
concession (use as is) or after rework count as first-pass failures. Trial lots from engineering and NPI runs are
reported separately and excluded unless requested.

## 3. Nonconformance reports

Every lot that fails first inspection is raised on an NCR and dispositioned as rework, scrap or use as is by the
material review board. A defect code with more than ten NCRs in a quarter requires an **8D corrective action**
with containment within 24 hours.

## 4. Cost of poor quality

**Cost of poor quality (COPQ)** is recorded on the NCR: scrapped material and labour, rework labour at the standard
rate and investigation effort. Aerospace nonconformances carry non-destructive testing and customer notification
effort and are typically several times more expensive. Warranty cost is reported separately.

## 5. Defects per million

Defects per million is defects found per million units inspected. It complements first-pass yield: a lot with one
defect and a lot with twenty both fail first time, but they contribute very differently to defects per million.

## 6. Field warranty

Shipped lots are tracked for warranty claims. The **warranty claim rate** is claims per 1,000 units shipped. Only
lots shipped more than **90 days** before the reporting date are included, because younger lots have not completed
their early-life window and would understate the rate. In 2026 hydraulic pumps showed a seal compound problem that
roughly tripled their claim rate; seal leaks dominate those claims.

## 7. Records

Inspection, NCR and warranty records are retained for the regulated life of the product, at least ten years for
aerospace parts.
