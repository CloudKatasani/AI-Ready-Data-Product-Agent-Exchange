# Sales Pipeline and Forecasting Playbook

## 1. Purpose

This playbook defines how Cobalt Cloud Software's sales organisation records opportunities, measures conversion
and forecasts bookings. Revenue operations use these definitions in the forecast call, and the Pipeline &
Bookings data product implements them.

## 2. Opportunity types

Every opportunity has exactly one type:

- **New business** — a first subscription for an account that has never paid.
- **Expansion** — additional seats or modules for a paying account, sold before its renewal date.
- **Renewal** — the renewal of an existing subscription at the end of its term.

Renewals are **retention, not new bookings**. They close at a much higher rate than new business, so including
them inflates win rate and makes the sales funnel look healthier than it is.

## 3. Stages

Open opportunities move through Discovery, Solution design, Proposal and Negotiation. An opportunity is closed
when it reaches its close date as Closed won or Closed lost. The expected close date of an open opportunity is
maintained by the account executive and reviewed weekly.

## 4. Conversion metrics

- **Win rate** — won opportunities divided by opportunities closed (won plus lost) in the period, by close
  date. Open opportunities are never in the denominator. Renewals are excluded unless the question asks to
  include them.
- **New bookings** — annual contract value of won new-business and expansion opportunities.
- **Average deal size** — mean annual contract value of won opportunities.
- **Sales cycle** — mean days from creation to close for won opportunities.

## 5. Pipeline

**Open pipeline** for a period is the amount of open opportunities whose expected close date falls in that
period. The forecast call reviews open pipeline for the next quarter by region, segment and stage. A healthy
pipeline for the next quarter is at least three times the quarter's bookings target.

## 6. Lead sources

Lead source records how the opportunity was generated: Outbound (sales development), Inbound (marketing),
Partner (resellers and alliances) or Event. Partner-sourced opportunities historically close at a higher rate
and are prioritised for co-selling.

## 7. Data handling

Sales rep names are personal data and are masked in analytical products for anyone without PII clearance.
Rankings by rep are used for coaching and capacity planning only, never as the sole basis of a performance
decision. Compensation and quota disputes are handled by sales operations, not through analytical tools.
