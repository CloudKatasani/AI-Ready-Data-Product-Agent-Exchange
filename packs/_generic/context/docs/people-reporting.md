# Workforce Reporting and Disclosure Standard

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines how Acme reports headcount, attrition and hiring, and how it protects the people behind the
numbers. It applies to the Workforce Composition data product, every people dashboard and every agent answer about
the workforce.

## 2. Who counts

Headcount and attrition count **permanent employees only** (BR-GEN-007). Contractors and interns are engaged for
fixed periods and turn over by design; including them would roughly double the attrition rate and hide the
retention problems that matter. A report may include them only when it says so explicitly.

## 3. Definitions

- **Headcount** is the number of permanent employees employed at month end, averaged over the months in the period.
- **Attrition rate** is leavers in the period as an annualised share of average headcount: leavers divided by
  employee-months, multiplied by twelve.
- **Voluntary attrition** counts resignations only; dismissals, redundancies and retirements are involuntary.
- **Time to hire** runs from the day a requisition is opened to the start date of the person who fills it
  (BR-GEN-012). Engineering requisitions take about a month longer than other functions.
- **Internal fill rate** is the share of hires filled by moving an existing employee into the role.

## 4. Disclosure control

Workforce data is personal data. Published figures are aggregates only, and **no figure is published for a group
of fewer than five people** (BR-GEN-008); smaller groups roll up to the next level. Names, emails and individual
records are never available through the Workforce Composition product or its agent, whoever asks. Questions about
a named employee are declined and referred to the HR business partner.

## 5. Current themes

Customer Support has the highest voluntary attrition in the group, and Engineering resignations rose sharply in
2026 as competitors increased hiring. Both are tracked in the quarterly people review.
