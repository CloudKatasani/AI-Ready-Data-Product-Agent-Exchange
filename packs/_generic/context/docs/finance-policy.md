# Group Financial Reporting Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Acme Holdings reports revenue, cost, margin and performance against budget across its
sixteen operating companies. It applies to the monthly management pack, the quarterly business reviews, every
dashboard built on the Financial Performance data product and every answer an agent gives about group results.
The aim is simple: the same question returns the same number wherever it is asked.

## 2. Revenue recognition

Revenue is recognised on the posting date of the invoice journal, in the legal entity that delivered the goods
or services. Credit notes are posted as separate negative journals and are never netted into the original line,
so the audit trail of every sale stays intact. Revenue is reported in US dollars at the monthly average rate.

## 3. Intercompany eliminations

Acme's legal entities trade with each other. The EMEA treasury and shared-services hub recharges group services,
licences and management fees to the other companies, and manufacturing entities sell components to the consumer
companies. These intercompany sales are real in each entity's own books, but they are not revenue to the group:
the selling entity's revenue is the buying entity's cost.

**Group revenue, cost and margin therefore exclude intercompany lines** (rule BR-GEN-001). A report that includes
them must say so explicitly, and is used only for entity-level statutory work. In the third quarter of 2026 the
EMEA hub booked an unusually large volume of recharges while the new shared-services model went live; including
them would overstate EMEA's revenue and change the regional ranking.

## 4. Margin definitions

- **Gross margin** is net revenue less cost of sales, divided by net revenue (BR-GEN-003).
- **Cost of sales** covers materials, freight, direct labour and hosting for software products.
- **Operating expense** covers selling, marketing, engineering and finance and administration.
- **Operating margin** is net revenue less cost of sales and operating expense, divided by net revenue.

## 5. Budget and variance

The annual budget is approved by the board in December by legal entity, month, account type and cost center.
Variance to budget is actual less budget, as a share of budget. A positive revenue variance is favourable; a
positive operating-expense variance is adverse. Any regional variance beyond plus or minus five percent in a
quarter needs a written explanation from the regional finance manager in the monthly business review
(BR-GEN-004). Budgets are not re-phased during the year; forecasts are a separate process.

## 6. Ownership

The Group Chief Financial Officer owns this policy. The Financial Performance data product is the single
certified source for the figures it defines; spreadsheet extracts from the ledger are not an acceptable source
for management or board reporting.
