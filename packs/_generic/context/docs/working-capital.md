# Credit, Collections and Payables Standard

## 1. Purpose

Working capital is cash the group has earned but not yet collected, minus cash it owes but has not yet paid.
This standard defines how Acme measures it — days sales outstanding (DSO), overdue receivables, days payables
outstanding (DPO) and supplier payment timeliness — so that treasury, regional finance and agents use one
definition.

## 2. Customer terms and collection

Standard customer terms are net 30 to net 45 days from the invoice date. Collections specialists contact
customers from the day after the due date. Decisions to put an account on hold, offer a payment plan or refer a
debt are always taken and recorded by a named person; analytical products and agents report receivables in
aggregate only and never recommend action on an individual customer.

## 3. Days sales outstanding

DSO measures how long customers take to pay, weighted by invoice value: each customer invoice contributes its
amount multiplied by its days to settle, and the total is divided by the total invoice amount (BR-GEN-006).
Days to settle runs from the invoice date to the payment date; an invoice still open at the reporting date counts
the days elapsed so far.

**Invoices in formal dispute are excluded from DSO and from overdue receivables** (BR-GEN-005). They are worked by
the disputes team and reported separately until resolved, because a disputed invoice is a quality or delivery
problem, not a collection problem. Including them roughly adds a week to group DSO.

Customers of the Latin American companies pay about three weeks slower than the rest of the group; regional DSO
should be read with that in mind.

## 4. Payables

Supplier invoices are paid on their due date — never early unless a prompt-payment discount is offered, and never
late (BR-GEN-011). Supplier payment timeliness is the share of paid supplier invoices settled on or before the due
date. DPO is calculated like DSO, on supplier invoices. From April 2026 facilities invoices have been paid late
after a change of accounts-payable team; this is tracked as an improvement action.

## 5. Ownership

The Group CFO owns this standard; the Working Capital data product is its certified source.
