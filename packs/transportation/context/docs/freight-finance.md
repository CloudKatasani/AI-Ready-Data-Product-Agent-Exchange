# Freight Finance Reporting Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines the revenue, cost and claims measures Meridian reports to its board and lenders: freight revenue,
cost per mile, revenue per loaded mile, operating ratio and the claims ratio.

## 2. Revenue

**Freight revenue** is linehaul plus fuel surcharge plus billed detention, recognised on the pickup date of the
load. Fuel surcharge follows the published diesel index with a one-week lag.

## 3. Cost per mile

**Cost per mile** divides operating, fuel and accessorial cost by **total miles — loaded plus empty**. Dividing by
loaded miles only is a common shortcut that hides the cost of empty repositioning. The diesel spike in the third
quarter of 2026 lifted fuel cost per mile across the network.

## 4. Revenue per loaded mile

**Revenue per loaded mile** is revenue, including fuel surcharge, per loaded mile — the yield on the miles the
shipper pays for.

## 5. Operating ratio

**Operating ratio** is operating cost, including cargo claims at reserve, as a percentage of revenue. Below 100% the
operation makes money; the plan is the low 90s.

## 6. Cargo claims

The **claims ratio** counts paid and open cargo claims at their reserve amount, excluding denied claims, as a share
of revenue. Claims are attributed to the pickup period of the load. Food and beverage shippers carry the highest
claims ratio because a temperature excursion spoils a whole reefer load. Claim decisions are made by a named claims
manager; no agent approves, denies or settles a claim.

## 7. Shipper contact data

Shipper logistics contacts (name, email and phone) are personal data. They are masked for every persona without
PII clearance and must have masking attached before any product that exposes them is certified.
