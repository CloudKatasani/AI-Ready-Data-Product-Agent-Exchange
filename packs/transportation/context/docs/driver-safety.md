# Driver Safety and Retention Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines how Meridian measures driver safety and retention, and how driver-level data may be used. It
covers company drivers and owner-operators leased on to Meridian.

## 2. Safety incident rate

A **DOT-recordable crash** is a crash involving an injury, a fatality or a vehicle towed from the scene. The
**safety incident rate** is recordable crashes per million miles driven. Telematics alerts (hard braking,
speeding), roadside inspection findings and non-recordable crashes are tracked for coaching but are not incidents.

The safety review board rules on every recordable crash; a **preventable crash** is one the driver could reasonably
have avoided. The preventable crash rate is reported per million miles.

A region whose recordable crash rate exceeds 2.0 per million miles over 12 months gets a safety stand-down and a
route review within 30 days. Mountain grades and winter chain-up routes make the Mountain region the one to watch.

## 3. Hours of service

Every **hours-of-service violation** recorded by the electronic logging device or found at a roadside inspection
is entered in the safety register within 24 hours.

## 4. Driver turnover

**Driver turnover** is annualised separations — voluntary and involuntary — as a share of average driver headcount.
It is reported for **company drivers only** unless a question explicitly asks to include owner-operators, because
owner-operators are independent businesses whose lease terminations follow different economics and are tracked
separately. Including them raises the reported turnover substantially.

## 5. Use of driver-level data

Driver names, phone numbers and licence numbers are personal data and are masked for anyone without PII clearance.
Driver-level safety data is used by the safety team for **coaching only**. Coaching, discipline and dismissal
decisions are made by a named safety manager on the driver's full record; no agent ranks, recommends or takes
action against an individual driver.
