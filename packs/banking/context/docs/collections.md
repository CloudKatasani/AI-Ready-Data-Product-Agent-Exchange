# Collections and Hardship Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Ridgeline Bank works delinquent loans: when an account enters collections, how
borrowers are contacted, how hardship is handled and how outcomes are measured. Collections are fair,
explainable and decided by people.

## 2. Entry to collections

A loan enters collections when it is 30 days past due. Cases are opened in the 30-59, 60-89 or 90+ DPD
bucket depending on when the borrower was first contacted.

## 3. Contact strategies

Cases are worked through digital self-service (secure messaging and online payment plans), the contact
centre, field collections for secured commercial loans, or a third-party agency for late-stage balances.

## 4. Promises to pay

A **promise to pay** is a borrower's commitment to pay a stated amount by a stated date. It is kept only
when the full promised amount arrives on time. Digital self-service promises are kept markedly more often
than promises taken by phone.

## 5. Outcomes

A case is resolved when the loan **cures** (returns to current), moves to a **payment plan**, or is
**charged off**. Cure rate and roll rate to charge-off are measured over resolved cases only, because
recently opened cases have not had time to resolve.

## 6. Hardship

Borrowers in documented hardship may be offered a payment plan of up to twelve months. Customers in
hardship or with protected status are never escalated automatically.

## 7. Customer contact data

Collectors contact borrowers using details in the customer master. These details are personal
information and are masked in analytical data products for anyone without PII clearance.

## 8. Reporting

Collections performance is reported monthly by strategy, entry bucket, segment and market in aggregate.
