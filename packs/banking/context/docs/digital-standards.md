# Digital Banking Service Standards

## 1. Purpose

These standards define how Ridgeline measures engagement and service quality on the mobile app and
online banking, and the targets the digital product team is held to.

## 2. Digitally active customers

A customer is **digitally active** when they log in to the mobile app or online banking at least once in
the last 30 days. The digital active rate is measured over active customers only.

## 3. Login success

Login success rate is the share of sessions with a successful authentication. The target is 96% or
better. A release that drops login success below 90% for a day is rolled back. In September 2026 an
authentication release reduced login success for three days before it was reverted.

## 4. Mobile share

Mobile share is the share of digital sessions on the mobile app. The Great Lakes market completed a
mobile-first branch redesign in 2025 and leads the bank on both mobile share and digital adoption.

## 5. Features

Sessions are classified by the main feature used: balance check, transfers, bill pay, mobile deposit,
card controls, statements and person-to-person payments.

## 6. Privacy

Session data is personal information. Products expose sessions in aggregate by market, channel, feature
and segment; device identifiers and IP addresses never leave the digital platform.
