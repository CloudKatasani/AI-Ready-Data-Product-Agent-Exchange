# ALCO Liquidity and Deposit Pricing Policy

## 1. Purpose

The Asset-Liability Committee (ALCO) manages Ridgeline's funding, liquidity and interest-rate risk. This
policy defines the deposit measures ALCO reviews monthly and the thresholds that trigger action.

## 2. Total deposits

Total deposits are the **average of month-end ledger balances** across the months in the reporting
period, by market, branch, product and segment. Balances are reported from the certified Deposits &
Liquidity product; spreadsheet extracts from the core are not used in the ALCO pack.

## 3. Deposit mix and funding cost

Checking (demand deposit) balances are **non-interest-bearing**; savings, money market and time deposits
are interest-bearing. A higher non-interest-bearing share lowers funding cost. **Cost of deposits** is
annualised interest expense divided by deposit balances.

## 4. Uninsured deposits

Balances above the $250,000 deposit insurance limit are **uninsured**. Uninsured deposits are the most
rate- and confidence-sensitive funding. When the uninsured share exceeds 30% of deposits, Treasury runs a
liquidity contingency review and reports to the board risk committee.

## 5. Pricing actions

Time-deposit promotions are approved by ALCO with a target volume and a maximum rate. During 2026 a
certificate-of-deposit promotion was used to replace rate-sensitive money market outflows in the
Southeast market. Promotional balances are monitored monthly for run-off.

## 6. Liquidity stress

The liquidity coverage ratio and 30-day stressed outflows are calculated by Treasury outside this
product. Deposit balances by product and insured status are the main inputs.

## 7. Confidentiality

Individual depositor balances are confidential. Analytical products and agents report deposits in
aggregate by market, branch, product and segment only.
